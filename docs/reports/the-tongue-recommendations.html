The Tongue · Eight Recommendations

From the game-design analysis: each mechanic shown as the game today (before) against a mocked-up version with the mechanic in place (after). Ordered by theme delivered per unit of engineering. Branch names and word forms are illustrative, in the game's own phonology.

1

The seed text

Impact: highEffort: trivial

A fixed proverb composed from lexicon concepts at world gen, rendered per branch every generation. Pure display code over existing state; the one artefact that tells the whole story at a glance.

3

Drift momentum

Impact: highEffort: small

Applying a rule tilts that branch's future autonomous drift towards its category (capped ×2, decaying each turn). The core verb becomes an investment; branches acquire characters. Proposed answer to the 2STK.1 stakes spike.

Before
Intervocalic voicingApply · 2
voiceless stop → voiced / V _ V
ApocopeApply · 2
vowel → ∅ / _ #

A menu of equivalent one-off edits: your pick versus the RNG's, nothing more.

After
Intervocalic voicingApply · 2
voiceless stop → voiced / V _ V
lenition momentum ×1.6
ApocopeApply · 2
vowel → ∅ / _ #
deletion momentum ×0.9
Aenic · the tongue that softens — lenition-tilted for 4 more generations

Sapir's drift as a mechanic. You steer the current; the RNG only floats on it.

6

A world that pushes back

Impact: highEffort: medium → large

Cheap tier: seeded generational events, chiefly edge-passability flips (a pass opens or freezes), which create fracture and contact on demand. Expensive tier, later: a rival autonomous family giving expansion an opponent and borrowing a direction.

Before
last gen: Aenic drifted (apocope) · Boran spread

Static edges, no rivals. Once free land runs out, half the verb set dies and the world goes quiet.

After
❄ The high pass freezes
The road between Naukes and Moatil closes.
Aenic will fracture at generation's end unless it abandons the far valley.
☼ A court rises at Moatil
Boran speech gains prestige.
Neighbours begin borrowing from it; its words spread along the trade roads.

One boolean flip per event, and the map starts generating drama instead of storing it.

7

The chronicle

Impact: mediumEffort: small

The end-of-turn log becomes narrated, era-named prose. Fracture and assimilation are the most consequential events in the simulation; at present they surface as one grey line. Doubles as the run's exportable history.

Before
last gen: Boran fractured → Kielan · Aenic assimilated into Boran

Two civilisational catastrophes, one line of grey text, gone on the next click.

After
Generation 12. In the mountains the speech of Boran broke in two; the northern tongue, now called Kielan, took to softening its stops. And Old Aenic was heard no more, though its words remain on the map at Nouɣes.

A string-template module, and the simulation's existing richness finally becomes audible.

8

Loss with residue

Impact: mediumEffort: medium

A dead branch's final lexicon is already frozen in the tree; let living branches spend influence to revive a dead relative's word, the learned-borrowing move (Latin into Romance, the Cornish revival). Waits on 2LEX.2 so collisions give it a mechanical reason to exist.

Death recolours a node. Nothing is lost that you valued, so nothing was really at stake.

Three roadmap edits

Unblock 2STK.1 now. The stakes spike is the most important unstarted task in the project; its dependency on the borrowing implementation (2GEO.5) is soft. Momentum + ambitions is the proposed answer.
Seed text and chronicle before glyphs. 2GLY is four tasks of spectacle that will land far better inside a game that already has stakes; these two deliver more theme per week.
Retire the economy panel from the player surface. Keep it behind a dev flag; replace with difficulty presets once the economy purchases consequences.
